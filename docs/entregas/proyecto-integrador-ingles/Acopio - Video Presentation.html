Introduction

After a disaster, people bring help to a collection center

In Spanish: centro de acopio. That is where our name comes from.

Background

Each center keeps its own records, so help can go to the wrong place

What we saw

Problem question

How can a web platform support the coordination and traceability of donations in kind between collection centers and affected areas during the response to a disaster?

Coordination
Centers and areas share what they have and what they need.
Traceability
We can follow a donation from start to finish.

Objectives

Develop a web platform that supports the coordination of donations in kind

  1. Define what each user needs
  2. Record inventory and follow donations
  3. Suggest transfers, with approval before dispatch
  4. Publish verified information and volunteer shifts
  5. Test the prototype and document its limits

Justification

The purpose is a shared record of donations

Project development

Four steps, and a person approves the transfer

  1. SuggestionThe system proposes what to move, from where and to where.
  2. ApprovalThe Administrator can change the quantity before approving.
  3. DispatchThe load leaves the center, linked to a donation code.
  4. ReceiptThe Receiver confirms what arrived and attaches a photo.
